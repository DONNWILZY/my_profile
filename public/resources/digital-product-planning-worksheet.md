## 1. The person and the problem

- Who is the primary person this product will help?
- What are they trying to do, and in what situation?

- How do they currently solve it?
- What have you observed or heard directly? Separate evidence from assumptions.

- What task will you ask them to attempt?

- What feedback would make you change or stop the idea?

## 6. Prepare a useful release

- [ ] The core journey works from beginning to end.
- [ ] Forms explain requirements, validation, and errors clearly.
- [ ] Loading, empty, success, and failure states are understandable.
- [ ] The interface works on a small screen and with a keyboard.
- [ ] Text is readable, controls have labels, and contrast is sufficient.
- [ ] Data collection is limited to what is needed and explained clearly.
- [ ] External services, links, and important dependencies are checked.
- [ ] There is a practical way to get help or report a problem.
- [ ] Someone owns maintenance, feedback review, and the next release.
